# What you can ask the room pod

Say **"Computer"** first, then your request: *"Computer, what time is it?"*
While you're talking with it (and for about 8 seconds after it answers) you can carry on without saying "Computer" again.

## How to talk to it

| You want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Ask something | "Computer, …" and speak normally |
| Follow up | Just keep talking: *"and tomorrow?"*, *"make it shorter"* |
| Stop it talking | Talk over it clearly, or tap a button |
| Change your mind mid-answer | Talk over it: *"No, actually, a unicorn"*. It knows what it was saying. |
| Make it stop listening | Double-tap a button (the ring turns magenta). Double-tap again to resume. |
| Call staff without speaking | Hold a button for 2 seconds |

**The light ring:** green = listening · amber = thinking · blue = speaking · magenta = muted · blinking blue = connecting.

## Questions it answers

| Topic | Try saying |
|---|---|
| Time | *"What time is it?"*, *"What time is it in Tokyo?"* |
| Weather | *"What's the weather like?"*, *"Will it rain in Paris tomorrow?"* |
| Distance | *"How far is it to the airport?"*, *"How far is Munich from Vienna?"* |
| News | *"What's in the news?"*, *"Any tech headlines?"* |
| Sports | *"How did Bayern play?"*, *"Any football scores today?"* |
| Public holidays | *"Is tomorrow a holiday?"*, *"When is the next bank holiday?"* |
| Fuel prices | *"How much is petrol here?"* |
| Horoscope | *"What's the horoscope for Leo?"* |
| Anything else | *"Tell me a story about a dragon"*, *"Explain black holes simply"*, *"Give me a pasta recipe"*, *"What should I see in Munich?"* |

It remembers the last few things you said during a conversation, so *"what about tomorrow?"* or *"tell me more"* work. For a hotel stay where you agreed to memory, it also remembers things you told it earlier in the stay.

## Room requests (sent to the staff desk)

Each request becomes a ticket for the staff, and the pod tells you it was passed on.

| Kind | Try saying |
|---|---|
| Housekeeping | *"Can someone clean the room?"*, *"Extra towels please"*, *"Two more pillows"*, *"I need toothpaste"* |
| Laundry and iron | *"Please pick up my laundry"*, *"Can I get an iron?"* |
| Food and drink | *"I'd like a club sandwich and water to the room"* |
| Room settings | *"Set the room to 22 degrees"*, *"Turn off the lights"*, *"Close the curtains"*, *"Switch on the TV"*, *"Some quiet music please"*, *"Do not disturb"* |
| Wake-up call | *"Wake me at 7"* |
| Bookings | *"Book a table for two at 8"*, *"Book a massage"*, *"Get me a taxi to the airport at 4"* |
| Information | *"What time is breakfast?"*, *"What's the Wi-Fi password?"*, *"Recommend a restaurant nearby"* |
| Checkout | *"Can I check out late?"*, *"I'd like to check out"*, *"What's my bill so far?"* |
| Problems | *"The shower is broken"*, *"I lost my glasses"*, *"I want to make a complaint"*, *"I'm locked out"* |
| Language | *"Can someone help me in Spanish?"* |

Room settings (temperature, lights, curtains, TV, music) are passed to the staff as requests; the pod does not switch them itself.

## Good to know

- **It only listens after "Computer"** (or while you're already talking with it). Ring green = it's listening.
- **Speak at a normal volume, facing the pod.** To interrupt an answer, speak a little firmer than usual; the button always works.
- **If it can't get something** (a service is down, a place it doesn't know), it says so and asks you to try again.
- **It never takes other actions by itself.** If it can't pass on a request, it says so; nothing is ordered or booked unless it tells you the staff got it.

## Not available on the room pod

Timers, reminders, alarms (other than a wake-up call through staff), messages, email, calendar, your own music, calculator, currency conversion, and translation. For a wake-up call or a taxi, ask and the staff will handle it.
